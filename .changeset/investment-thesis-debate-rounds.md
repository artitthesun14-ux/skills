`investment-thesis`: the investment debate now runs as rounds driven by questions. Each exchange names its core question, the assumption under test and a status. Each round ends on the key question the next round takes up, every turn names its attack target and answers the turn before it, and evidence states what it proves and does not prove. Concessions record what changed, either side can change position, and each exchange closes on What did we learn. The verdict reads Strengthened, Unchanged, Weakened, Unresolved or Broken with at most three reasons, followed by What changed. The page reference adds round indicators, a pinned core question, key-question separators and thesis-impact marks, keeping the editorial committee look.
