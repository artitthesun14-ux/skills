# Thesis scorecard: [Company]

One row per dimension, and no overall score: the dimensions trade off, and the user weighs them.

**Assessment** uses High / Medium / Low, or a short qualitative phrase where a level misleads (e.g. "High but cyclical"). For Valuation, the assessment describes how demanding the embedded expectations are. For Bear Case Strength, High means the Bear proved a lot in the debate.

**Confidence** is how well the evidence supports the assessment: High (Tier 1 sources, cross-checked), Medium (some inference or Tier 2 to 3 sources), Low (mostly assumption).
