# Counter-thesis and thesis breakers

Two separate outputs. The **counter-thesis** is the strongest coherent argument that the thesis is wrong. A **thesis breaker** is a specific observable event that would force you to revise or abandon it.

## Counter-thesis

Write it as the bear's best case, with its own causal chain:

```
THESIS:         AI increases demand for power infrastructure.
COUNTER-THESIS: AI efficiency improves faster than expected and reduces infrastructure demand.
```

Search for real disconfirming evidence across:

- Technology substitution.
- Demand slowdown.
- Supply expansion (competitor and new-entrant capacity).
- New competitors.
- Customer insourcing.
- Margin compression.
- Regulation.
- Capital cycle (overbuilding after the shortage).
- Commoditization.

Then test the thesis **claim by claim**. For each claim the thesis rests on, write one card: the claim, the evidence for it and the evidence against it facing each other, the key unknown between them, and the thesis breaker that would settle it. A claim with no evidence against it yet is not countered, it is an **unchallenged assumption**: label it so and name the evidence that would fill the gap.

Search for it with the same effort you spent on confirming evidence: query the bear case directly, read short reports and competitor filings, and note which supportive sources have an interest in the story. Rate the counter-thesis **Low / Medium / High** probability with the evidence behind the rating.

## Thesis breakers

List each breaker with a monitoring indicator and a threshold:

| Breaker | Indicator | Threshold | Source to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market share decline | Share in core segment | Down two years running | Industry data, peer filings |
| ROIC below cost of capital | ROIC versus WACC | Below for a full year | Annual report |
| Pricing power gone | Price realisation versus input costs | Negative for two quarters | Earnings calls |
| Major customer insourcing | Top-customer capex and hiring | Announced in-house program | Customer filings |
| Superior substitute | Substitute cost and adoption | Cost parity reached | Technical documentation |
| Bottleneck relieved | Industry lead times | Back to pre-shortage level | Industry associations |
| Industry economics deteriorate | Industry margins, capacity utilisation | Structural decline | Peer filings |

Use the breakers that fit this company, and add company-specific ones. Each one goes into the thesis tracker.
